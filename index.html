How to read the map

Each star is a club; each coloured glow is an interest area. Click an area (its name, glow or any star) to fly in, then click a cluster to open it full-pane with every club named. Click a club's name or dot for details. The chips above pick an area, one at a time.

Inside a cluster, lines join clubs whose descriptions are similar. Bigger, brighter stars have a description, a clear way to join and more related clubs. Size never reflects member counts or selectivity.

Nearby clubs share interests; the map doesn't show shared members or formal affiliations.

Independent student project, not affiliated with Harvard. Details come from public directory pages and club websites; topics are inferred and may be imperfect. Ranking reflects how well a club matches your search, never size or selectivity.
