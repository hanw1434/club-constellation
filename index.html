How to read the map

Each star is a club. Glowing clusters are interest areas: select an area's name to fly in and see its sub-areas, then a sub-area to zoom closer.

Lines join clubs whose descriptions are similar. Bigger, brighter stars have a description, a clear way to join and more related clubs. Size never reflects member counts or selectivity.

Nearby clubs share interests; the map doesn't show shared members or formal affiliations.

Independent student project, not affiliated with Harvard. Details come from public directory pages and club websites; topics are inferred and may be imperfect. Ranking reflects how well a club matches your search, never size or selectivity.
